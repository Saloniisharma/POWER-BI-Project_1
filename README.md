# POWER-BI-Project_1

# Airbnb Listings Dashboard

## Problem Statement

The data analytics industry has seen rapid growth, leading to an increasing demand for professionals in various roles such as Data Scientists, Data Engineers, and Data Analysts. However, there is a lack of comprehensive insights into the factors that influence the job satisfaction, compensation, and challenges faced by individuals in these roles. This lack of clarity affects both aspiring and current data professionals in making informed career decisions, as well as employers aiming to attract and retain talent.

This Power BI dashboard aims to address the following key issues:

Understanding Job Satisfaction: Analyze the overall happiness of data professionals with their work-life balance and salary.
Evaluating Career Entry Barriers: Identify the perceived difficulty of entering the data field, which could help in formulating strategies to reduce these barriers.
Compensation Insights: Provide insights into average salaries across different job titles to help individuals and organizations benchmark compensation.
Geographical Distribution: Examine the distribution of data professionals across different countries to identify geographic trends.
Technical Preferences: Highlight the most popular programming languages among data professionals to guide both educational curriculum development and skill-building initiatives.
By addressing these areas, the dashboard seeks to provide valuable insights to stakeholders within the data industry, enabling more informed decision-making and fostering a more supportive environment for data professionals.


### Steps followed 

- Step 1 : Load data into Power BI Desktop, dataset is a csv file.
- Step 2 : Open power query editor & in view tab under Data preview section, check "column distribution", "column quality" & "column profile" options.
- Step 3 : Also since by default, profile will be opened only for 1000 rows so you need to select "column profiling based on entire dataset".
- Step 4 : It was observed that in none of the columns errors & empty values were present but we have add a new column named "AVG Salary".
- Step 5 : Two card visuals were added to the canvas, one representing average age of delay survey takers & other representing total survey takers.
- Step 6 : Two bar charts were also added to the report design area representing the Average salary of different Data Professionals & their favourite programming languages.
- Step 7 : A doughnut chart shows the percentage distribution of respondents' perceptions of how difficult it is to break into the data industry. Categories include "Very Easy," "Easy," "Neither Easy nor Difficult," "Difficult," and "Very Difficult," providing a clear understanding of perceived entry barriers.
- Step 8 : A treemap displays the geographic distribution of survey participants. Countries such as the United States, India, Canada, and the United Kingdom are highlighted, along with an "Other" category. This visualization helps identify where data professionals are primarily located.
